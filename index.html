MARKET CORRELATION GUIDECommon macro relationships to monitor
GOLD ↔ USDOften inverse

A stronger USD can pressure dollar-priced gold, but real yields and safe-haven demand also matter.

GOLD ↔ REAL YIELDSOften inverse

GOLD ↔ RISK AVERSIONRegime-dependent
